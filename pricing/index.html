Pricing: One Package, Three Levels

How we arrive at your number.

Most bookkeeping stops at a closed month. Every level below gets you clean, CPA-reviewed books and a cash system that sets aside taxes and profit before you spend. The only thing that changes as you move up is how often we sit down with you.

Three things drive the number: how many accounts and transactions you have, whether you carry inventory, and how far behind the books are. A clean set of books on one bank account is not the same job as eighteen months of catch-up across four accounts with inventory, so quoting you before we have looked would either overcharge you or set us both up for a bad conversation later.

So we look first. The $497 Diagnostic Review is where that happens: we go inside your QuickBooks, document what is actually there, and quote a flat monthly fee from what we find. The $497 is credited toward your first month, so if you come on board it costs you nothing net.

Which level is right for you?

Same package at every level. The difference is how often we sit down together.

Foundations

Includes: Clean, CPA-reviewed books, the cash system, quarterly strategy meetings, and your business tax return, prepared and filed. That covers whatever your business files, a Schedule C, an S corp, or a partnership.

Who it’s for: Owners who want clean, reliable books and the cash system running, with a check-in each quarter. A good fit when the business is steady and you don’t need frequent strategy time.

Meetings: Quarterly strategy meeting.

Move up when: you have questions between quarters, or you’re making bigger decisions (hiring, equipment, a new sales channel) and want a monthly set of eyes.

Growth

Includes: Everything in Foundations, plus your personal tax return, with estimated payments handled. You and your spouse filing jointly count as one return. If your business has more than one owner or partner, each additional owner’s personal return is quoted separately.

Who it’s for: Owners actively growing who want a monthly conversation about the numbers, not just a report. Most of our clients land here.

Meetings: Monthly strategy meeting.

Move up when: decisions are happening weekly, cash is tight or lumpy, or you want a CPA on call rather than on a schedule.

Partner

Includes: Everything in Growth, plus room for a second entity.

Who it’s for: Owners who want their CPA close in, with priority access and the most frequent cadence. Best for more complex or faster-moving businesses.

Meetings: Bi-weekly meetings, plus priority access between them.

What we don’t do (so there are no surprises).

We would rather be clear up front than have you find out later.

  • ✗ We are not an audit or attest firm. We don’t issue audited or reviewed financial statements.
