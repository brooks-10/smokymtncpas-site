Common questions

Tax questions.

Do you prepare and file tax returns?

Yes. We prepare and file business and personal returns. Every monthly plan includes the business return and one owner’s personal return. If you only need this year’s return filed, we do that too.

What business returns do you file?

A Schedule C, an S corp (1120-S), or a partnership (1065), plus your personal return. They are prepared and filed by the CPAs who keep the books.

Is my tax return included with monthly bookkeeping?

Yes. Monthly clients get their business return and one owner’s personal return, prepared and filed from the books we keep. A married couple filing jointly counts as one. Each additional owner’s personal return is quoted separately.
