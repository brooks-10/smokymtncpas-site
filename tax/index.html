Fair Questions

Answers before you ask.

What do you need from me to start a return?

Last year’s return and this year’s documents. We send you one short checklist, you upload everything once, and the tracker shows you the rest.

Is the price on the calculator the price I pay?

It is the starting price for that kind of return, and it is what most clients pay. We confirm it in writing after we read last year’s return, before any work starts. If your situation turns out to be more involved, you hear the number first.

Do I have to move my bookkeeping to you for the monthly plans?

Yes, and that is the point. The quarterly numbers, the estimates, and the planning all work because we keep the books they come from. Return-only clients keep their own books and we prepare from your records.

I already have a tax preparer I like.

Then keep them. We work alongside good preparers all the time and refer clients to them. This page is for owners who do not have one, or whose taxes and books have never talked to each other.

How late can I start and still make the deadline?

The honest answer depends on your entity and how clean your records are. Send us last year’s return and we will tell you the same day whether we can file on time or whether an extension is the smarter move. An extension is a filing deadline, not a payment deadline, and we walk you through what to pay with it.

Do you handle personal returns without a business?

Yes, through the Individual Tax Membership: your return prepared and filed, a mid-year checkup with a withholding review, and we handle IRS letters if they show up. Starting at $595 a year, or starting at $995 with rentals or K-1s.
