Fair questions

Do I have to move my bookkeeping to you for the monthly plans?

Yes, and that is the point. The quarterly numbers, the estimates, and the planning all work because we keep the books they come from. Return-only clients keep their own books; we prepare from your records.

I already have a tax preparer I like.

Keep them! We work alongside good preparers all the time and refer clients to them. This page is for people who do not have one, or whose taxes and books have never talked to each other.

What do you need from me to start a return?

Last year's return and this year's documents. We send you one short checklist, you upload everything once, and the tracker shows you the rest.
