You’re a great fit if…

The owners we click with.

  • ✓ You want clean, CPA-reviewed books and a cash system that tells you what you can pay yourself, not just a P&L.
  • ✓ You value getting it right over getting it cheapest. You understand two CPAs cost more than an overseas data-entry service, and you want the difference.
  • ✓ You’re on QuickBooks Online, or willing to move to it.
  • ✓ You want to pay yourself properly and stop being surprised by taxes.
  • ✓ You’re ready to hand the books off and get back to running the business.

Who we’re not the right firm for.

We’d rather tell you now than three months in.

  • ✗ You’re shopping purely on price. We’re not the cheapest and never will be.
  • ✗ What you really need is someone to file your taxes. We don’t prepare or file tax returns. Here’s how that works →
  • ✗ You want a full audit or reviewed financial statements. We’re not an audit or attest firm.
  • ✗ You need deep, detailed job-costing software built out. We handle the bookkeeping and cash side cleanly, but that’s a different service.
