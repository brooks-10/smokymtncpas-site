A common question

Why we don’t prepare taxes.

Yes, we’re CPAs. No, we don’t file tax returns, and that’s a choice, not a gap. Here’s the honest reasoning, and exactly how we work alongside your tax preparer so filing is painless.

The short version.

Focus makes us better. Bookkeeping and cash-flow strategy is a full craft on its own, and doing it well year round is different work from preparing tax returns. By not splitting our attention with tax season, every client gets clean books, a working cash system, and same-day answers all twelve months, not just the busy ones.

You still end up in a better spot at tax time, because your books are ready every month and your tax money is already set aside.

Questions owners ask us.

Do you prepare or file my tax returns?

No. We focus on monthly bookkeeping and cash strategy. We keep your books accurate and tax-ready all year so your tax preparer’s job is simple, but we don’t prepare or file the returns themselves.

Why not, if you’re CPAs?

Because focus makes us better. A firm that tries to be everything to everyone usually does none of it especially well. We chose bookkeeping and cash strategy, and we go deep on it. That’s why you get a pay-yourself-first cash system and same-day answers, things most tax-focused firms don’t offer.

How do you work with my tax preparer?

Hand in hand. We give your tax preparer clean, reconciled, CPA-reviewed books and year-end financials, and we answer their questions directly so nothing stalls. You’re not stuck in the middle relaying messages. If you don’t have a tax preparer, we can point you to someone good.

Will I still be ready at tax time?

More ready than most. Your books are tax-ready every month, not reconstructed in a spring scramble. And because our cash system sets aside your tax money as you go, the bill itself is never the surprise it is for most owners.

What if I really just need someone to do my taxes?

Then we’re honestly not your firm, and we’ll tell you that early rather than waste your time. We’re the right fit for owners who want their books and cash handled at a high level, working alongside a tax preparer, not instead of one.
