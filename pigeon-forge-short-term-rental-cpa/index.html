The one local thing that trips owners up

Inside the city, or in the county?

This is a jurisdiction check, not a filing position. We confirm before anything is remitted.

The local catch: whether your cabin sits inside Pigeon Forge city limits or in unincorporated Sevier County can change where your lodging tax is filed. Plenty of owners are not sure which side of the line they are on, so we check it first.

Inside Pigeon Forge city limits it is handled one way, and in unincorporated Sevier County another, with different rates and filing points. So the first thing we do is confirm which jurisdiction applies to your cabin, before anything is remitted. For the full picture of who remits and when, see our short-term rental tax answers.

Answers before you ask.

Do you work with out of state Pigeon Forge cabin owners?

Yes. Many of our cabin clients live outside Tennessee. We keep the books remotely and a CPA answers when you reach out.

Does where my cabin sits in Pigeon Forge change my tax filing?

It can. Whether your cabin is inside Pigeon Forge city limits or in unincorporated Sevier County can change where your local lodging tax is filed, so we confirm which applies before anything is remitted. See our short-term rental tax answers for the detail.

What does per-cabin bookkeeping include?

Each cabin gets its own profit and loss, its own expense lines, and its loan split into principal, interest, and escrow. You see what every cabin actually earns.
