Profit First Coaching

Working this hard and still not paying yourself? That’s the problem we fix.

You haven’t taken a real paycheck in months. The tax bill shows up and the money isn’t there. Revenue is up, and somehow there’s nothing to show for it. You check the bank balance before every purchase because you honestly don’t know what’s safe to spend. None of that means your business is broken. It means nobody ever built you a system that pays you first.

The system we coach is called Profit First. The mechanics are simple: the moment income lands, it gets split into set percentages. Profit. Your pay. Taxes. Operating money. You run the business on what’s left, not on the whole pile.

  • ✓ Cash flow you can predict

Who it’s for

Built for owners who are tired of flying blind.

If your business is making money but you never seem to have any, you’re not alone, and it’s not a math problem. It’s a system problem.

One client of ours hadn’t run a real paycheck for themselves in over a year. The business was profitable the whole time. Every dollar just looked spoken for, so “pay myself” kept losing to “cover this month.” Two months after we set up their allocations, they ran their own payroll. Nothing about the business changed. How it works

A system, not a spreadsheet you’ll abandon.

Three steps turn cash chaos into a calm, predictable routine you can actually stick to.

Set up your accounts

We open a handful of purpose-driven bank accounts: Profit, Owner’s Pay, Taxes, and Operating Expenses. Every dollar has a job the moment it arrives.

Allocate by percentage

On a simple rhythm, income gets divided into those accounts by target percentages tailored to your business, not a generic template.

Run on what remains

You spend from Operating Expenses with total clarity. Profit, pay, and taxes are already handled. No more end-of-quarter surprises.

Profit First questions, answered.

What is Profit First?

Profit First is a cash system that pays you first. The moment income lands, it gets split into set percentages for profit, your pay, taxes, and operating money, and you run the business on what is left.

How does Profit First work in practice?

It works in three steps. We open a handful of purpose driven bank accounts (Profit, Owner's Pay, Taxes, and Operating Expenses), income gets divided into them by target percentages tailored to your business, and you spend from Operating Expenses knowing profit, pay, and taxes are already handled.

Is Profit First included in your bookkeeping?

It is an optional add on alongside our monthly bookkeeping, not a standalone service. Allocations only work when the numbers underneath them are real, so the cash system always runs on clean books reviewed by a CPA.

Why do Profit First with a CPA?

Because a CPA connects your cash system directly to your tax picture. Your tax savings percentage is based on real projections, not a guess, and we prepare and file the return from the same books.
