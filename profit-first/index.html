How it works

A system, not a spreadsheet you’ll abandon.

Three steps turn cash chaos into a calm, predictable routine you can actually stick to.

🏦 Set up your accounts

We open a handful of purpose-driven bank accounts — Profit, Owner’s Pay, Taxes, and Operating Expenses — so every dollar has a job the moment it arrives.

📊 Allocate by percentage

On a simple rhythm, income gets divided into those accounts by target percentages tailored to your business, not a generic template.

📈 Run on what remains

You spend from Operating Expenses with total clarity. Profit, pay, and taxes are already handled — no more end-of-quarter surprises.
