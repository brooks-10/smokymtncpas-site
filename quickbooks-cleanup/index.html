What actually gets fixed

The things that make a file “messy.”

Nine problems account for most of the cleanups we take on. Some of these are quietly changing your profit number right now.

Accounts that have never been reconciled

Every bank, credit card, and loan account matched to the actual statement, month by month, instead of to itself.

Transactions parked in Uncategorized

The catch-all buckets get emptied and coded properly, with real vendor knowledge behind the calls, not a guess.

Duplicates and missing transactions

Double-entered deposits, transactions that never imported, and transfers booked twice as income and expense.

Opening Balance Equity that was never cleared

A balance sitting in that account is a sign the file was set up and then abandoned mid-setup. It gets traced and closed out.

Undeposited Funds that keeps filling up

Payments recorded but never matched to the deposit that actually hit the bank, which quietly inflates both income and receivables.

Payouts booked as revenue

Shopify, Stripe, Square, and marketplace payouts split into sales, fees, refunds, and sales tax, instead of one lump number that overstates your income.

Inventory and cost of goods that never move

Purchases expensed straight to the P&L with no cost of goods, which makes every margin on your reports wrong.

Owner money mixed with business money

Draws, contributions, loans, and payroll separated so the equity section means something and your return starts from the right number.

Vendors not set up for 1099s

Contractor payments identified and vendor records corrected before January, not during it.

How far behind can I be?

Years behind is normal and it is fine. We have picked up files that had not been reconciled since the account was opened. The further back it goes the more months there are to rebuild, which is what drives the quote, but there is no point where it becomes too late to fix.

Do I have to sign up for monthly bookkeeping afterward?

No. The Diagnostic Review and the cleanup both stand on their own, and plenty of people have us clean the file up and then take it from there. The $497 credits toward the cleanup if you move ahead with it.

Will I have to amend past tax returns?

Sometimes. If the cleanup changes a year that has already been filed, we will show you exactly what changed and walk you through your options. Amendments are quoted separately from the cleanup.

Do you need access to my bank accounts?

We start with read-only access to your QuickBooks. We do not move money and we do not need the ability to. For the cleanup itself we will need your bank and credit card statements for the months being rebuilt, so every account can be reconciled to the statement rather than to itself.

Do you only work with businesses in Knoxville?

We are based in Knoxville, Tennessee and work with clients across East Tennessee and nationwide. Cleanup work runs entirely remotely through secure document sharing and read-only access.
