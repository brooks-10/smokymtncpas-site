What we actually review

We go past the statements, into the account.

Most reviews skim your financial statements and stop. We go inside QuickBooks and look at how things are actually set up, categorized, and recorded, because that’s where the real problems hide.

Financial statement review

We examine your P&L, balance sheet, and transaction history for accuracy, inconsistencies, and anything that could cost you at tax time.

Deep dive into your QuickBooks

We go beyond the surface numbers and look at how your account is really set up, categorized, and reconciled, down to the details.

Written findings report

A document detailing every issue found, every correction needed, and exactly where your books stand right now. Yours to keep.

Personal Loom video walkthrough

We record ourselves walking through your actual review, so you can see what we found, hear our reasoning, and pause or rewind any time.
