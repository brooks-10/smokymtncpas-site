Shopify Bookkeeping Pricing

Here's exactly what we charge — and exactly how we calculate it.

Most bookkeeping firms make you sit through a sales call before they’ll tell you what they charge. We think that’s backwards. Below is our complete pricing — every revenue band, every complexity tier, and an honest explanation of what drives your number. Use it to estimate where your store falls.

01

Revenue drives your base price

The more revenue your store does, the more financial activity there is to track. More Shopify payouts, more transactions, more complexity at tax time. Revenue sets the floor for your price before we know anything else about your store.

02

Complexity moves you within your band

Four things shift you toward the lower or higher end of your range:

  • Sales channels — Shopify only vs. multi-channel
  • Payment portals — each one beyond Shopify Payments adds reconciliation work
  • Transaction volume — 150/mo vs. 2,000/mo is a different job
  • Accounting method — accrual is significantly more complex than cash basis

Your monthly rate — all-inclusive, flat fee.

Every price below includes Shopify bookkeeping, CPA review, P&L and balance sheet delivery, and A2X software. No hourly billing. No surprise invoices. Find your revenue band and complexity level to estimate your range.
