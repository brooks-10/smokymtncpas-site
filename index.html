Before you reach out

We're not the right fit for everyone. Here's who shouldn't hire us.

✗ You're looking for the cheapest bookkeeper you can find.

Our pricing reflects two licensed CPAs, multiple levels of review, and real communication. If price is the only variable, we're not your firm. We'd rather tell you that now than waste your time on a call.

✗ You just want a tax return filed once a year.

We're a monthly bookkeeping and cash-strategy firm, not a tax-prep shop. If all you need is an April filing and no help the other eleven months, a tax preparer will serve you better.

✗ You want someone to quietly process transactions and stay out of your way.

We're invested in your business. We flag things. We ask questions. We notice when something doesn't look right. If you want silence, we're going to frustrate each other.

✗ You need someone who can take you on regardless of fit.

We cap our roster intentionally. If we don't think we're the right match, we'll tell you, and point you somewhere better. We'd rather lose the client than take on work we can't do well.

Common Questions

Answers before you ask.

How much does monthly bookkeeping cost?

Three levels, one package: Foundations at $800/month with quarterly strategy meetings, Growth at $1,000/month with monthly meetings, and Partner at $1,500/month with bi-weekly meetings and priority access. Every level includes clean, CPA-reviewed books and the pay-yourself-first cash system.

Do you file tax returns?

No. We're a monthly bookkeeping and cash-strategy firm. We keep your books tax-ready all year and hand your preparer a clean file, which usually makes their bill smaller too.

What is the $497 In-Depth Diagnostic Review?

A one-time deep dive into your QuickBooks by two licensed CPAs: a written findings report plus a recorded video walkthrough of what we found and what it means. The full $497 is credited toward your first month if you come on board, and it carries a 30-day money-back guarantee.

My books are months behind. Can you fix that?

Yes, catch-up and cleanup work is routine for us. We start with the Diagnostic Review so you know exactly what's broken and what it will take to fix before you commit to anything.

Do you only work with Shopify stores?

Shopify and ecommerce books are our deepest specialty, but we bring the same system to retail shops, contractors, and other small businesses. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you on the first call and point you somewhere better.

Where are you located? Do you work remotely?

We're based in Knoxville, Tennessee and serve clients nationwide. Everything runs remotely: secure document sharing, video meetings, and read-only bank access.
