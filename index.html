Quick answers.

What does it cost?

It depends on your accounts, transactions, inventory, and how far behind the books are, so we quote after we look, not before. The $497 Diagnostic Review is where that happens, and it’s credited toward your first month.

Do you do taxes?

Yes. We prepare and file both business and personal returns, from the same books we keep all year.

My books are months behind. Can you fix that?

Yes. Cleanup is where we start for a lot of clients; the Diagnostic Review tells us how far behind and what it takes.

Do you only work with one industry?

No. We go deep in a few niches (real estate investors, ecommerce and retail sellers, and small businesses) so we catch what generalists miss, but we serve small businesses across East Tennessee and nationwide.
